    On-campus support

    • Locked out of your classroom or another facility emergency: Stanford Land, Building, and Real Estate, 650-723-2281.
    • Non-emergency security concern: Stanford Dispatcher, 650-329-2413, 24 hours a day.
    • Emergency: 911. From a University phone, dial 9-911.
    • Classroom technology trouble mid-class: post in the Slack channel help-csp-instructors.
